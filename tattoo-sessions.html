Before You Pick A Time Block
Not sure which one?

Select “Not sure — recommend one” on the inquiry form. Jessie will recommend the block that makes the most sense.

The deposit applies to your total.

Scheduled-session deposits are 50% of the session price and are what officially reserve the appointment.

One session can hold more than one tattoo.

The question is whether the approved work fits safely and reasonably inside the amount of time you booked.

More work than time?

Anything that cannot be completed within the reserved block moves to another session instead of being rushed.
