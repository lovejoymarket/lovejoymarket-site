Questions Before You Buy The Thing You Already Want
Where does my payment go?

Square. LoveJoyMarket.co does not collect or store card numbers.

Will there be pickup?

Whatever that item offers will show on its Square checkout page. Some things may be pickup only, some may ship, and I will not make you guess.

What about preorders?

Each preorder will say when it closes, when I expect it to be ready, how you will get it, and whether anything is still due later.

I have an order question.

Email hello@lovejoymarket.co with the name on the order and the Square receipt details. I cannot solve a mystery order with no clues, babe.
