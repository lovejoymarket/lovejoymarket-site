Questions Before You Buy Something You Absolutely Need
Where does my payment go?

Square. LoveJoyMarket.co does not collect or store card numbers.

Will there be pickup?

Pickup or shipping options will be shown on the Square checkout for the specific item when available.

What about preorders?

Preorder windows, production estimates, pickup/shipping details, and any final-payment instructions will be stated on the product and Square checkout.

I have an order question.

Email hello@lovejoymarket.co and include the name used at checkout plus your Square order/receipt details.
