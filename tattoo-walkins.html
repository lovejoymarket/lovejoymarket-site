Walk-In Saturdays
✦★✧♡✦★✧
2ND+4TH SATURDAY OF EVERY MONTH

$100 flat. Walk-In Saturdays are for quick, easy tattoos intended to take about 30–45 minutes, with roughly one hour allowed per person so nobody has to be rushed.

Walk-in means walk-in: no advance reservation and no deposit. Arrive, check in, and availability is handled in order based on the actual flow of the day.

Second and fourth Saturdays are reserved for walk-in flow, not full-day appointments.

Walk-In Rules At A Glance
Keep it quick.

These are intentionally shorter tattoos. If the idea needs more planning, detail, or time, you may be asked to book a scheduled session instead.

No reservation required.

You do not need to send the regular tattoo inquiry just to come to a Walk-In Saturday.

$100 flat for the walk-in format.

Valid LoveJoy coupons personally issued to you can still be used according to their terms.

Normal client rules still apply.

You still need valid ID, informed consent, and to arrive able to be tattooed safely. No feet or intimate-area tattoos.

Need Something Bigger?

If your idea is not really a quick walk-in tattoo, use the regular inquiry and Jessie can point you toward the right scheduled session.
