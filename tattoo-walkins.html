✦★✧♡✦★✧
2ND+4TH SATURDAY OF EVERY MONTH

$100 flat. These are quick, easy tattoos that should take about 30–45 minutes. I leave roughly an hour for each person because racing a tattoo is deeply unserious.

Walk-in really means walk-in. No reservation and no deposit. Check in when you arrive, and I take people in order as the day moves.

Second and fourth Saturdays belong to walk-ins. Full-day tattoos have several other perfectly good days to choose from.

The Tiny Rules
Keep it quick.

If the idea needs a long design conversation, a pile of detail, or more time, I will send you to a scheduled session. It is not a punishment.

No reservation required.

You do not need to fill out the regular inquiry just to come on Walk-In Saturday. Please enjoy the rare absence of paperwork.

$100 for the walk-in format.

A valid LoveJoy coupon that I personally gave you can still be used according to its terms.

Normal client rules still apply.

Bring valid ID, be able to consent, and arrive safe to tattoo. I still do not tattoo feet or intimate areas, even on a Saturday.

Got A Bigger Crush?

If you already know the idea is too involved for a walk-in, send the regular inquiry. I will tell you which appointment fits without making you guess.
