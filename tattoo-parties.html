About the guest signup list

Rather than asking you to guess how many people will want tattoos weeks in advance, we’ll start a list once the date is confirmed. Guests just add their name. That’s it.

We’ll check in on the list around two weeks before the party to work out tattoo timing. I’ll talk through designs with participants separately. People can still ask to join later if there is room; a name on the list is a request, not a guaranteed appointment.

The host keeps the names together and shares the list with me privately. No tattoo details, contact information, or medical information needed on the sheet.

A few things your guests should know
Everyone getting tattooed must be 18+.

Each person brings a valid government-issued photo ID and completes their own consent paperwork.

Party tattoos are smaller projects.

We can talk through flash, a shared theme, or simple custom ideas. Bigger work and complex cover-ups are better booked as a regular session.

You can absolutely have food and drinks.

We just need to keep them away from the tattoo station. I won’t tattoo anyone who is impaired.

Read the full party policies for rescheduling, safety, promotion, and everything else. They cover the details for both private and public parties.
