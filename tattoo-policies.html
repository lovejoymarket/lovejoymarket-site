The Rules That Keep Us Cute
50% deposit · regular sessions

Regular appointments need a 50% deposit through Square. It comes off your total and makes the appointment official. Pay your deposit here ♡

Recuérdame · November 1, 2026 only

This approved memorial appointment is $75 total, with a $25 nonrefundable deposit applied and $50 due at your appointment. Pay only after your tattoo details and time are approved. Pay the $25 Recuérdame deposit ♡ · Event details + inquiry

One reschedule

One reschedule is permitted, subject to availability. Further reschedules require a new deposit. Offers with specific appointment dates apply only within their stated dates.

Cancellation / no-show

Deposits are nonrefundable. A new booking payment is required to book again after a cancellation or no-show.

Private address

Appointment location + arrival details are provided after payment.

One guest maximum

Please limit your appointment to one guest total and let me know beforehand if someone is joining you. Children count toward this limit.

Bringing children

Appointments are best attended without children, but I understand that bringing your child may sometimes be necessary. Please let me know in advance and bring everything needed to keep them comfortably occupied throughout your session. The studio is not set up to entertain children.

Arriving early

Please do not arrive more than 15 minutes before your appointment unless I have contacted you and specifically said I can take you early. The suite stays locked and is not a public waiting space, so I may not be available to let you in before then.

Running late

You have a 15-minute grace period without contacting me. If you are running later than that, please message me. At 30 minutes past your appointment start time, I leave and the appointment is canceled. You will not be charged the remaining tattoo balance, but a new deposit is required to book again.

Complete consent first

Finish your intake and consent before arrival.

Work within the block

I reserve the amount of time you booked. If the tattoo needs more, we schedule the rest.

Body-area limits

LoveJoy does not tattoo feet or intimate areas.

Come able to consent

Do not arrive impaired.

Before Your Appointment

Eat a meal, hydrate, and wear clothing that gives access to the tattoo area. Do not shave the area or apply numbing cream beforehand. Bring your government-issued photo ID and complete your consent form before arrival.

Questions or scheduling changes: hello@lovejoymarket.co
