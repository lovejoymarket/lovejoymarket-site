The Rules That Keep Us Cute
50% deposit

Regular appointments need a 50% deposit through Square. It comes off your total and makes the appointment official. Pay your deposit here ♡

One reschedule

You get one reschedule before a new deposit is needed.

Cancellation / no-show

Deposits are nonrefundable. A new deposit is required to book again after a cancellation or no-show.

Private address

Appointment location + arrival details are provided after payment.

Complete consent first

Finish your intake and consent before arrival.

Work within the block

I reserve the amount of time you booked. If the tattoo needs more, we schedule the rest.

Body-area limits

LoveJoy does not tattoo feet or intimate areas.

Come able to consent

Do not arrive impaired.
