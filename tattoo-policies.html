The Rules That Keep Us Cute
50% deposit

Regular appointments need a 50% deposit through Square. It comes off your total and makes that time officially yours.

One reschedule

Things happen. Tell me as soon as you can. You get one reschedule before a new deposit is needed.

Cancellation / no-show

Deposits are nonrefundable. If you cancel or miss the appointment, the deposit is gone and a new one is required to book again.

Private address

The building address is public. The suite number and how to get in come with your confirmed appointment details.

Complete consent first

Finish your intake, medical questions, and consent before you arrive. Bring the ID the form asks for.

Work within the block

I reserve the amount of time you booked. If the tattoo needs more, we schedule the rest. I am not rushing good work to win an argument with a clock.

Body-area limits

LoveJoy does not tattoo feet or intimate areas.

Come able to consent

Do not arrive impaired. I can pause or refuse tattooing if there is a problem with consent, health, safety, or sanitation.

Questions belong in email

Send booking questions, changes, and paperwork to hello@lovejoymarket.co so neither of us has to excavate six different DMs later.
