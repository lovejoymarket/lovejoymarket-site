Tattoo appointment details

All times are Eastern. Requested times are subject to confirmation.

One guest maximum. Please let me know beforehand if someone is joining you. Children count toward the one-guest limit.

Appointments are best attended without children, but I understand that bringing your child may sometimes be necessary. Please bring everything needed to keep them comfortably occupied throughout your session. The studio is not set up to entertain children.

Your tattoo must fit within the session you book. Additional work may need another appointment. Feet and intimate areas are not tattooed.

Eat beforehand, hydrate, and wear clothing that gives access to the tattoo area. Please do not shave the area or apply numbing cream. Bring your government-issued photo ID and complete your consent form before arrival.
