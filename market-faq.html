Before You Send Me A “Quick Question”
The event says full. Should I still apply?

Yes. Send the right form from the Applications page. If I approve you, I can add you to the waitlist in case somebody drops out.

Do I pay when I submit?

No. Fees change by event, and an application does not reserve anything. If I accept you, the email will say exactly what the fee is and how to pay it.

Can my friend take my booth if I cancel?

No. Tell me as soon as you cancel and I will offer the opening through the waitlist. Your friend is welcome to apply like everybody else.

I provide a service. What documents do you need?

Attach the current permits, licenses, insurance, safety training, or certifications required for what you do. If you are not sure, send what you have and explain the service in plain language. I will tell you what is missing.

What do food or beverage vendors send?

Start with the Vendor / Maker application. Add product and setup photos plus the current permits or food-safety documents for what you plan to serve. I cannot finalize the spot until the required paperwork checks out.

Do featured artists pay to hang work?

No. Featured artists do not pay a participation or display fee. Use the Featured Artist application. We will agree on installation, sales, any commission, and payout timing before the work touches the wall.

Can I pitch something that does not fit a category?

Yes. Use the Creative / Community Partner application. Tell me what you want to do, what you bring, what you need from me, and whether the idea comes with special space or legal requirements.

Where do I send general questions?

hello@lovejoymarket.co. Questions, forms, attachments, and “this is weird but hear me out” all go there.
