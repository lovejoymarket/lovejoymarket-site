Frequently Asked Little Situations
The event says full. Should I still apply?

Yes. If your application is approved, you can be considered for the waitlist if space opens.

Do I pay when I submit?

No. Vendor fees vary by event. A completed application never reserves a space by itself. If you are accepted, LoveJoy sends the event-specific fee and official payment instructions.

Can my friend take my booth if I cancel?

No. Spaces belong to the event and cannot be transferred. Contact LoveJoy immediately and the opening goes back through the waitlist.

I provide a service. What documents do you need?

Attach the current permits, licenses, certifications, insurance, safety training, or other documentation required for the service you plan to perform. If you are not sure what LoveJoy needs to verify, send what you have and explain the activity clearly.

What do food or beverage vendors send?

Start with the vendor application, product/setup images, and the current permits or food-safety documentation applicable to what you plan to serve. Final confirmation can be held until required documents are verified.

Do featured artists pay to hang work?

No. LoveJoy Market does not charge featured artists a participation or display fee. Installation, sales handling, commission if any, and payout timing are agreed before the work goes on the wall.

Can I pitch something that does not fit a category?

Absolutely. Use the Creative + Partner application and explain what you want to do, what you provide, what LoveJoy would need to provide, and any compliance or space needs.

Where do I send general questions?

hello@lovejoymarket.co. Same inbox for questions, forms, attachments, and “this idea is weird but hear me out.”
