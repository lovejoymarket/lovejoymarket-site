Which Party Are You?

Both party types use the same tattooing standards and the same $250 reservation deposit. The difference is who the event is for, who is expected to promote it, and what LoveJoy is agreeing to provide publicly.

PRIVATEVS.CO-PROMOTED
invite-only

Private, Cute + Invite-Only

Who it is for

An invite-only group. This is the better fit for birthdays, friend groups, small celebrations, bridal groups, private gatherings, or any party where the host already knows who is coming.

Who handles attendance

The host creates the guest list, invites people, communicates the tattoo-party rules, and keeps the group organized. LoveJoy is not responsible for filling the room.

LoveJoy promotion

Public promotion is not included or required. LoveJoy may repost, mention, or share the party at Jessica’s discretion, but that is a bonus rather than a promised marketing package.

Host promotion

The host can share privately or publicly with invited guests. If the party is posted publicly, the event information and tattoo pricing must still be accurate.

Artist credit

Any public post that mentions the tattooing should identify the artist as Jessica LoveJoy / @amoajessie. LoveJoy Market should be credited when the venue/business is being referenced.

Pricing language

Every tattoo has a $50 minimum. That does not mean every tattoo costs $50. Final pricing depends on the actual design, placement, detail, and time required.

Best choice if…

You already have your people and mainly need the private tattoo-party experience, not a public marketing collaboration.

public-facing

Co-Promoted + Outside

Who it is for

A party or pop-up intentionally promoted beyond a private guest list. This is the better fit for brands, community events, public celebrations, collaborations, or events where turnout matters.

Who handles attendance

The host and LoveJoy share promotional responsibility. The host is still expected to bring their own audience and actively promote instead of relying on LoveJoy to generate the crowd.

LoveJoy promotion

Included organic promotion may include a listing on the LoveJoy website where appropriate, one main social or collaboration post, up to two Story reminders, and reasonable reposting of useful host-created content.

Host promotion

The host must provide complete event details and usable assets early enough to promote, actively share the event to their own audience, and keep public information accurate. Public promo should begin only after the party is approved and the reservation deposit is paid.

Artist credit

Public event descriptions, flyers, captions, ticket/RSVP pages, and promotional graphics that mention tattooing must clearly identify Jessica LoveJoy / @amoajessie and LoveJoy Market.

Pricing language

Public promo must say “tattoos start at $50” or “$50 tattoo minimum.” The host may not advertise unlimited tattoos, guaranteed slots, unapproved discounts, or flat-$50 pricing unless Jessica approves that exact offer in writing.

Best choice if…

You want the tattoo party to function as a public-facing collaboration and both sides are prepared to actively promote it.
