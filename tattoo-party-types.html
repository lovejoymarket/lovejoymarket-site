Which Party Are You?

Both options use the same tattoo rules and the same $250 reservation deposit. The real question is simple: is this for people you already invited, or are we both promoting it to the public?

PRIVATEVS.CO-PROMOTED
invite-only

Private + Invite-Only

Who it is for

Birthdays, friend groups, bridal groups, small celebrations, or any gathering where you already know who is coming.

Who handles attendance

You make the guest list, invite everybody, share the rules, and keep your people organized. I am not responsible for finding guests for your private party.

What I post

Public promotion is not part of this option. I may repost or mention it if I feel like it, but treat that as a cute bonus, not a campaign I promised you.

What you can post

Share it privately or publicly with your invited guests. If you post it, keep the event details and tattoo prices accurate.

Tag the artist

If a public post mentions tattooing, credit Jessica LoveJoy / @amoajessie. Tag LoveJoy Market when you are talking about the space or business.

Say the price correctly

Tattoos start at $50. That does not make every tattoo $50. Design, placement, detail, and time still matter.

Pick this one if…

You already have your people. You want the party, not a joint public event and marketing plan.

open to the public

Co-Promoted + Public

Who it is for

Brands, community events, public celebrations, pop-ups, and collaborations where people beyond your private guest list are invited.

Who handles attendance

We both promote it. You still need to bring your own audience, because “LoveJoy will find everybody” is not a promo plan.

What I post

I may list it on the LoveJoy website, make one main or collaboration post, share up to two Story reminders, and repost useful content you create.

What you post

Get me the details and usable graphics early, post to your own audience, and keep the information right. Do not announce it until I approve the party and the deposit is paid.

Tag the artist

Anything public that mentions tattooing must clearly credit Jessica LoveJoy / @amoajessie and LoveJoy Market.

Say the price correctly

Use “tattoos start at $50” or “$50 tattoo minimum.” Do not promise unlimited tattoos, guaranteed spots, surprise discounts, or flat $50 pricing unless I approved those exact words in writing.

Pick this one if…

You want a public event and both of us are ready to actually tell people about it.

Okay, Which One Sounds Like You?

Check the booking numbers next. If you already know which one you want, you can skip ahead and tell me the plan.
