Client Policies
50% deposit

Scheduled sessions require a 50% deposit through Square. The deposit applies toward your session total and is what reserves the appointment.

One reschedule

If plans change, contact LoveJoy as soon as possible. One reschedule is allowed before a new deposit is required.

Cancellation / no-show

Deposits are nonrefundable. A cancelled or missed appointment forfeits the deposit and requires a new deposit to book again.

Private address

The building location may be public, but the suite number and appointment-specific arrival instructions are released with your confirmed appointment information.

Complete consent first

Your client intake, medical questionnaire, and tattoo consent must be completed before arrival. Bring the identification required by the consent form to the appointment.

Work within the block

Your session is a reserved time block. If the approved plan needs more time than remains, additional work moves to another session.

Body-area limits

LoveJoy does not tattoo feet or intimate areas.

Come able to consent

Do not arrive impaired. If there is a consent, health, safety, or sanitation concern, tattooing can be paused or declined.

Questions belong in writing

Email hello@lovejoymarket.co so there is a clean record of booking questions, changes, and paperwork.
