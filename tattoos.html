✦ TATTOO PARTY POLICIES the grown-up part. read it before paying anything.
$250 party reservation deposit

A $250 nonrefundable reservation deposit is required after the inquiry is approved. The deposit reserves the party date, planning, prep, and group scheduling. It does not pay for, reduce, or apply toward the cost of any tattoo. An inquiry alone never holds a date.

30-day booking minimum + final planning deadline

Tattoo parties must be booked at least 30 days before the requested date. The host should provide the final expected participant count and basic tattoo plans at least 14 days before the party. Last-minute additions or major design changes are not guaranteed.

Tattoo pricing + what fits a party

Every tattoo has a $50 minimum. $50 is the minimum, not a flat party price. Final pricing depends on design, size, placement, detail, and time. Party tattoos should be reasonably time-bounded. Large projects, complicated cover-ups, or work that needs extensive consultation may be redirected to a normal appointment. LoveJoy does not tattoo feet or intimate areas.

How many people can get tattooed?

There is no advertised unlimited tattoo count. Capacity depends on the number of participants, design complexity, placement, and the confirmed party window. Approval of the party does not guarantee that every late-added guest can be tattooed.

18+, ID, consent + sobriety

All tattoo-party participants receiving tattoos must be 18 or older, present valid government-issued photo ID, complete LoveJoy’s normal intake and consent process, and be able to give informed consent. Anyone who appears impaired or creates a health, consent, or safety concern will not be tattooed. Refusal of service for safety reasons does not create a refund or credit against the party reservation deposit.

Food, drinks + the tattoo area

The tattoo procedure area stays controlled during the party. Food, drinks, unnecessary traffic, and other contamination risks stay outside the tattoo work area. Party atmosphere does not override normal sanitation or safety rules.

Rescheduling, cancellation + LoveJoy cancellation

One reschedule is allowed with at least 14 days’ notice. The replacement date must be mutually agreed upon, subject to availability, and occur within 90 days of the original date. Rescheduling with less than 14 days’ notice, cancelling the party, or failing to appear forfeits the $250 reservation deposit and requires a new deposit to book again. If LoveJoy must cancel and cannot offer a reasonable replacement date, the host may transfer the deposit to a new date or receive the $250 back.

Arrival time + guest behavior

The reserved party window begins at the confirmed start time. Late arrivals do not automatically extend the event. LoveJoy may pause or end a party if guest behavior creates a sanitation, harassment, safety, or consent concern. The host is responsible for communicating party rules to invited guests.

Private Tattoo Party promotion

Private parties are invite-only. The host manages invitations and guest communication. LoveJoy is not required to publicly advertise the event, create promotional graphics, or generate attendance. LoveJoy may repost or mention the party at Jessie’s discretion. If the host posts the party publicly, the tattoo artist should be credited as Jessica LoveJoy / @amoajessie.

Co-Promoted Tattoo Party promotion

Co-Promoted parties are publicly advertised and use shared promotional responsibility. Included LoveJoy promotion may include a website listing where appropriate, one primary social or collaboration post, up to two Story reminders, and appropriate reposts of usable host-created content. Paid ads, custom campaign strategy, repeated graphic design, photography/video production, or additional promotional work are not included unless separately agreed upon.

Host promotion + artist credit for Co-Promoted parties

The host must provide complete event information and usable promotional assets no later than 21 days before the event, actively promote the event to their own audience, and keep public information accurate. Public event descriptions, social captions, flyers, ticket/RSVP pages, and other promotional material that mention tattooing must clearly identify Jessica LoveJoy / @amoajessie and LoveJoy Market.

No unauthorized promises + no turnout guarantee

The host may not publicly promise guaranteed tattoo slots, unlimited tattoos, unapproved designs, discounts, pricing, or artist availability. Public pricing should say “tattoos start at $50” or “$50 tattoo minimum.” LoveJoy branding, logo use, tattoo photos, artwork, or likeness must be approved before being used in public promotion. Promotion begins only after the party is approved and the reservation deposit is paid. Neither party guarantees attendance, ticket sales, tattoo sales, reach, or social performance.

Client Policies
50% deposit

Scheduled sessions require a 50% deposit through Square. The deposit applies toward your session total and is what reserves the appointment.

One reschedule

If plans change, contact LoveJoy as soon as possible. One reschedule is allowed before a new deposit is required.

Cancellation / no-show

Deposits are nonrefundable. A cancelled or missed appointment forfeits the deposit and requires a new deposit to book again.

Private address

The building location may be public, but the suite number and appointment-specific arrival instructions are released with your confirmed appointment information.

Complete consent first

Your client intake, medical questionnaire, and tattoo consent must be completed before arrival. Bring the identification required by the consent form to the appointment.

Work within the block

Your session is a reserved time block. If the approved plan needs more time than remains, additional work moves to another session.

Body-area limits

LoveJoy does not tattoo feet or intimate areas.

Come able to consent

Do not arrive impaired. If there is a consent, health, safety, or sanitation concern, tattooing can be paused or declined.

Questions belong in writing

Email hello@lovejoymarket.co so there is a clean record of booking questions, changes, and paperwork.
