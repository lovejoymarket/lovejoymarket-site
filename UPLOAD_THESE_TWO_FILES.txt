What it does:
- Adds a pink glittery old-school hit counter directly above the homepage footer.

- Increments once per homepage load.
- Polls the shared counter every 15 seconds so the displayed number can catch up when other visitors arrive.
- Uses CounterAPI v1 (public, authentication-free) for the shared count.
