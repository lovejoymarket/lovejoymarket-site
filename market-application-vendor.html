Your table space

Each indoor vendor space fits one six-foot table, with about two feet of space behind it for you and your chair. Include racks, extra displays and all other space needs in your inquiry. Extra space needs approval. Confirm your table / display plan with Jessie.

Food and open containers

No food or open containers in the event room. Food vendors must use an assigned hallway space, not a table inside the event room.

Food vendors and food trucks

Hallway food spaces are assigned individually. Keep displays, equipment and customer lines clear of exits and walkways. Include your menu, full setup dimensions, power needs and relevant documents in your inquiry.

Food truck and other outdoor setups are requests for review. They are not confirmed by this form. Wait for Jessie to approve your setup and location before making arrangements.

Event hours and setup

The market runs 7–11 p.m. Jessie will share load-in and breakdown instructions with accepted vendors. Keep your setup staffed, stay through your approved breakdown time, and leave your space clean.

Confirmation and payment

The vendor fee is $20 per approved business. An inquiry does not reserve a space. Pay only after Jessie confirms your participation. If you would like to be considered for the waitlist, select that option in the same inquiry form. There is no payment to request a waitlist spot.

The rules for every vendor

An application is not a spot.

You are not booked until I confirm you for that specific event.

Do not send money early.

Fees change by event. If I accept you, the email will tell you the amount, deadline, and real payment method.

You cannot give your spot away.

Do not sell it, trade it, split it, send a substitute, or tell a friend they can have it. The waitlist exists for a reason.

Bring what I approved.

Sell, serve, or perform only what was accepted. If the plan changes in a real way, ask first.

If it needs a permit, I need to see it.

Food, body art, cosmetology, alcohol, and other regulated work cannot happen until I verify the current required documents.

Stay in your space.

Keep exits, stairs, walkways, tattoo areas, and private areas clear. Your display may have personality. It may not have territorial ambitions.

You run your own checkout.

Bring your payment setup, receipts, bags, change, and whatever you need for your own taxes unless we agreed to something else.

Show up and clean up.

Arrive during your load-in window, keep the booth staffed, stay until approved breakdown, and leave the space the way you found it.

Safety ends the debate.

Harassment, threats, unsafe behavior, illegal products, unapproved flame or alcohol, or refusing a safety direction can end your participation immediately.

A specific event may add rules. Health, safety, licensing, insurance, lease, and legal requirements always win over convenience. Annoying sometimes, still true.

Flash tattoo details for your guests

Tattoo clients must be 18+ with government-issued photo ID. Walk-ins only, first come, first served; 13 client spots. Pre-drawn designs only, no customizations. $50 per tattoo, two tattoos maximum per person.
