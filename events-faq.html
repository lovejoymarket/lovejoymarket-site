Event FAQ
Are LoveJoy events all ages?

Not automatically. Age policy depends on the event. Check the confirmed event listing before assuming kids or minors can attend.

Do I need a ticket?

Some events are ticketed, some have a cover, and some are simply open. If payment or registration is required, the confirmed calendar listing will say so and provide the official link.

Where are the events?

LoveJoy Market events are based in Fishers, Indiana. Exact event logistics and any arrival instructions are included with the confirmed event information.

Can I vend at one of these?

Maybe. Vendor applications, featured-artist applications, guest tattoo clearance, workshops, and partner forms live on the Market page. Do not use the event-collaboration form to skip the application process.

Can I get tattooed during an event?

Only when tattooing is specifically part of that event. Check the listing or the Tattoo page for the current booking/walk-in setup.

Can I rent LoveJoy for my own event?

Send the Private Event Inquiry above. An inquiry does not hold a date; availability and the actual event terms have to be confirmed first.

I have an event idea but it is kind of weird.

That is not disqualifying. Use the LoveJoy Event / Collaboration Inquiry and explain the experience clearly enough that Jessie can picture how it would actually run.

Who do I email with a question?

hello@lovejoymarket.co. Keep event questions in email so there is one searchable record instead of six half-conversations across apps.
