Things You Were Definitely About To DM Me
Are LoveJoy events all ages?

Age guidelines depend on the host and format. Check the confirmed pop-up announcement before bringing the whole group.

Do I need a ticket?

Any ticket, RSVP, or entry details will come from the confirmed pop-up announcement or the host. No active LoveJoy event calendar is posted right now.

Where are the pop-ups?

Locations vary. Each confirmed pop-up will include its own venue, date, and arrival details. Do not assume a pop-up is at the tattoo studio.

Can I vend at one of these?

Tell me about your work through the collaboration form. Opportunities depend on the specific host, venue, and format.

Can I get tattooed during an event?

Only if tattooing is part of the confirmed pop-up. Regular tattoo appointments still begin on the Tattoos page.

Can LoveJoy come to my event?

Ask through the private pop-up inquiry. This is not a studio room-rental reservation.

I have an event idea but it is kind of weird.

Weird is allowed. Vague is harder. Use the collaboration form and explain it well enough that I can picture what people would actually do.

Who do I email with a question?

hello@lovejoymarket.co. Please keep event questions there so I am not piecing together six half-conversations from three apps.
