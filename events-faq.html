Things You Were Definitely About To DM Me
Are LoveJoy events all ages?

Not by default. Every event gets its own age rule, so check the listing before bringing a child and hoping for the best.

Do I need a ticket?

Sometimes. Some events have tickets or a cover, and some are just open. If you need to pay or RSVP, the calendar listing will say it plainly and give you the real link.

Where are the events?

LoveJoy events are based in Fishers, Indiana. The exact location details and anything special about getting in will be on the confirmed listing.

Can I vend at one of these?

Maybe. Vendor, featured artist, guest tattoo, workshop, and partner applications live on the Market page. The collaboration form is not a secret side door around them.

Can I get tattooed during an event?

Only if the event says tattooing is part of it. Check the listing or the Tattoo page before showing up with a screenshot and optimism.

Can I rent LoveJoy for my own event?

Use the Private Event Inquiry. Sending it does not hold the date. I still need to confirm the room, price, and plan first.

I have an event idea but it is kind of weird.

Weird is allowed. Vague is harder. Use the collaboration form and explain it well enough that I can picture what people would actually do.

Who do I email with a question?

hello@lovejoymarket.co. Please keep event questions there so I am not piecing together six half-conversations from three apps.
