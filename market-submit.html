How To Apply Without Creating Administrative Folklore
✦★✧♡✦★✧
1
Download the right PDF.

Choose your lane on the Applications page, then save its form to your device before filling it out. Do not close the browser tab like a beautiful little disaster.

2
Fill it out + save the completed copy.

Use Adobe Acrobat, Preview, Files, or another PDF app that preserves form fields. Reopen it once to make sure your answers actually saved.

3
Add the receipts.

Portfolio/product photos, booth examples, logo, permits, licenses, insurance, training certificates, food-safety docs, or other current documentation your activity requires.

4
Email one complete submission.

Send the completed PDF and all attachments to hello@lovejoymarket.co. Suggested subject: APPLICATION – type – your name/business.

5
Then wait for an actual yes.

Submitting an application does not reserve a spot, date, wall, booth, tattoo station, or workshop. If a fee applies, you will receive the amount and official payment instructions after acceptance.
