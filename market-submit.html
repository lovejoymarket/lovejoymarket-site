How To Send It Without An Email Saga
✦★✧♡✦★✧
1
Download the right PDF.

Choose the right form on the Applications page and save it to your device before you start. Filling it out inside a browser preview is how people end up yelling at a PDF.

2
Fill it out and save it.

Use Acrobat, Preview, Files, or another PDF app that keeps form fields. Close it and reopen it once to make sure your answers did not disappear for attention.

3
Add the proof.

Attach clear work or product photos, booth examples, your logo, and any current permits, licenses, insurance, training, or food-safety documents your activity requires.

4
Send one complete email.

Email the finished PDF and every attachment to hello@lovejoymarket.co. Use the subject APPLICATION | type | your name or business.

5
Wait for the actual yes.

An application does not hold a spot, date, wall, booth, tattoo station, or workshop. If there is a fee, the acceptance email will tell you the amount and how to pay it.
