Tiny Gallery Questions
Does submitting mean my photo will definitely be posted?

No. The gallery is curated manually. A submission is permission to review the photo, not a promise that it will be published.

Can I submit a healed tattoo even if Jessie already has a fresh photo?

Yes. Healed work is useful, and the gallery does not need to be a duplicate of the tattoo portfolio.

Can I ask you to remove a photo later?

Yes. Email LoveJoy with enough information to identify the image and request removal.

Why can't I upload directly to the website?

Because nothing from a public form should get to publish itself. Photos are reviewed first, then added manually.
