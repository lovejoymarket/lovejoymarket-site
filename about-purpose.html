The Point, Basically

I do not want LoveJoy Market to become a giant anonymous “creative brand.” I want it to stay specific enough that you can feel who made it.

That means good tattoos. Good art. Fair opportunities for artists. Events people actually want to attend. A space where local creative work is treated like culture instead of decoration. And enough flexibility to keep making the next weird thing before there is a tidy category for it.

The goal is not to be everything. The goal is to make the right things feel at home together.
