The Waitlist, Not The Friend Zone

If the event you want is full, submit the right application anyway.

Once I approve your application, I can add you to that event’s waitlist. I usually offer openings in order, but the spot still has to fit. A food vendor with power needs cannot replace a two-foot print table just because they emailed first.
