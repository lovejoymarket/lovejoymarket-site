The Waitlist Is A Real List, Not Purgatory

If the event you want is full, submit the application anyway.

Approved applicants can be placed on the event waitlist. Openings generally move in chronological order among approved applicants, but LoveJoy may move to the applicant who actually fits the opening when category, footprint, power, permit, or other event requirements matter.

more than 24 hrs before doorsan offered vendor will normally have about 2 hours to accept
within 24 hrsthe response window may shrink to about 30 minutes

If you cancel, your spot returns to LoveJoy Market. Do not recruit your own replacement. The opening goes back through the approved waitlist.
