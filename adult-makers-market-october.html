For Makers Who Want One Of The Six Spots
  • Working booth fee: $40 per business.
  • You set and keep your own activity and product prices.
  • Your core activity should be repeatable and finish in about 15–20 minutes.
  • Tell Jessie your setup, materials, price, reset time, and what you need from the room.
  • Email hello@lovejoymarket.co. Keep the whole conversation there.
