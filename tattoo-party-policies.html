Tattoo Party Policies
$250 reservation deposit

The $250 reservation deposit is due only after the inquiry is approved. It reserves the party date, planning, preparation, and group scheduling. It is nonrefundable once booked and does not pay for or reduce the price of any tattoo. An inquiry alone never holds a date.

30-day booking minimum + 14-day planning deadline

Tattoo parties must be booked at least 30 days in advance. The host should provide the final expected tattoo-participant count and basic tattoo plans at least 14 days before the party. Late additions or major design changes are not guaranteed.

Tattoo pricing + party-appropriate work

Every tattoo has a $50 minimum. Final pricing depends on design, size, placement, detail, and time. Party tattoos should be reasonably time-bounded. Large projects, complicated cover-ups, or work that needs extensive consultation may be redirected to a normal scheduled appointment. LoveJoy does not tattoo feet or intimate areas.

Capacity + late additions

The number of tattoos that can be completed depends on the confirmed party window and the approved designs. Booking a party does not create unlimited tattoo capacity, and late-added guests are not guaranteed a tattoo.

18+, ID, consent + impairment

Everyone receiving a tattoo must be 18 or older, present valid government-issued photo ID, complete LoveJoy’s normal intake and consent process, and be able to give informed consent. Anyone who appears impaired or creates a health, consent, or safety concern will not be tattooed.

Food, drinks + procedure area

The actual tattoo procedure area remains controlled during the party. Food, drinks, unnecessary traffic, and other contamination risks stay outside the tattoo work area.

Rescheduling + cancellation

One reschedule is allowed with at least 14 days’ notice. The replacement date must be mutually agreed upon, subject to availability, and occur within 90 days of the original date. Rescheduling with less than 14 days’ notice, cancelling the party, or failing to appear forfeits the $250 reservation deposit and requires a new deposit to book again.

If LoveJoy has to cancel

If LoveJoy must cancel and cannot offer a reasonable replacement date, the host may transfer the $250 reservation deposit to a new agreed date or receive that deposit back.

Arrival time + group behavior

The confirmed party window begins at the confirmed start time. Late arrivals do not automatically extend the event. LoveJoy may pause or end the party if guest behavior creates a sanitation, harassment, safety, or consent concern. The host is responsible for communicating the rules to guests.

Private-party promotion

Private Tattoo Parties are invite-only. LoveJoy is not required to advertise the party, create promotional graphics, or generate attendance. If the host posts publicly, the tattoo artist should be credited as Jessica LoveJoy / @amoajessie.

Co-Promoted LoveJoy promotion

Included organic promotion may include a LoveJoy website listing where appropriate, one primary social or collaboration post, up to two Story reminders, and reasonable reposting of useful host-created content. Paid advertising, custom campaign strategy, repeated graphic creation, photography/video production, or additional marketing work are not included unless separately agreed upon.

Co-Promoted host responsibility + artist credit

The host must provide complete event information and usable promotional assets early enough to promote, actively promote the event to their own audience, and keep public information accurate. Public descriptions, flyers, captions, ticket/RSVP pages, and promotional graphics that mention tattooing must clearly identify Jessica LoveJoy / @amoajessie and LoveJoy Market.

No unauthorized promises + no turnout guarantee

The host may not advertise guaranteed tattoo slots, unlimited tattoos, unapproved designs, discounts, pricing, or availability. Public pricing should say “tattoos start at $50” or “$50 tattoo minimum.” Promotion begins only after approval and payment of the reservation deposit. Neither party guarantees attendance, ticket sales, tattoo sales, reach, or social-media performance.
