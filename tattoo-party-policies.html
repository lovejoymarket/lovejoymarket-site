The Rules That Keep It Cute
$250 reservation deposit

You pay the $250 deposit after I approve the party. It holds the date and covers the planning and prep. It is nonrefundable once booked and does not pay for anyone’s tattoo. Filling out the form alone holds nothing.

30-day booking minimum + 14-day planning deadline

Book at least 30 days ahead. Send the final expected tattoo count and basic ideas at least 14 days before the party. Late additions and major changes get a “maybe,” not a promise.

Tattoo pricing + what works at a party

Tattoos start at $50. Final pricing depends on the design, size, placement, detail, and time. Large projects, complicated cover-ups, and anything needing a long consultation belong in a regular appointment. I do not tattoo feet or intimate areas.

How many people can get tattooed

That depends on how long the party is and what everybody wants. Booking the party does not create unlimited tattoo time, and guests added late are not guaranteed a spot.

18+, ID, consent + impairment

Everyone getting tattooed must be 18 or older, bring valid government-issued photo ID, complete the normal intake and consent, and be able to give informed consent. I will not tattoo anyone who is impaired or raises a health, consent, or safety concern.

Food, drinks + the tattoo area

Food, drinks, wandering guests, and anything else that could contaminate the setup stay outside the actual tattoo area. The party can party. My station cannot.

Rescheduling + cancellation

You get one reschedule with at least 14 days’ notice. We both have to agree on the new date, it depends on availability, and it must happen within 90 days of the original date. Less than 14 days’ notice, cancellation, or a no-show loses the $250 deposit and requires a new one to book again.

If I have to cancel

If I cancel and cannot offer a reasonable replacement date, you can move the $250 deposit to a new date we agree on or have it returned.

Start time + group behavior

The clock starts at the confirmed time. Late arrivals do not make the party longer. I can pause or end it over harassment, unsafe behavior, sanitation problems, or consent concerns. The host is responsible for giving the rules to the guests before they arrive.

Private-party promotion

A private party is invite-only. I do not have to advertise it, make graphics, or fill the room. If you post publicly, credit the tattoo artist as Jessica LoveJoy / @amoajessie.

What I promote for a co-promoted party

I may add a LoveJoy website listing, make one main or collaboration post, share up to two Story reminders, and repost useful content from the host. Paid ads, a full campaign, endless graphics, or photo and video production are not included unless we agree to them separately.

What the co-promoted host handles

Get me complete details and usable graphics early, actively post to your own audience, and keep the public information accurate. Anything that mentions tattooing must clearly credit Jessica LoveJoy / @amoajessie and LoveJoy Market.

Do not promise things I did not approve

Do not advertise guaranteed spots, unlimited tattoos, unapproved designs, surprise discounts, or availability I did not confirm. Public pricing must say “tattoos start at $50” or “$50 tattoo minimum.” Promotion starts after approval and the deposit. Neither of us can guarantee attendance, sales, reach, or social-media performance. The algorithm has never signed a contract.
