The basics
01 / BOOK AHEADAt least 30 days

Please ask about dates at least a month out so we can plan the setup and designs.

02 / DEPOSIT$250 to reserve

Once I approve your party, the nonrefundable reservation deposit holds the date and covers planning. It does not include tattoos.

03 / TATTOOSStarting at $50

Prices vary by design, size, placement, and time. I’ll review the requested designs before confirming each tattoo.

04 / PARTICIPANTSGuest signup list

Once the date is confirmed, invite anyone who wants a tattoo to add their name. No advance headcount or tattoo details needed on the list.

What happens after you reach out?
1

Send your idea and dates.

Tell me the occasion, preferred day, whether the event is private or public, and the kinds of tattoos you’re picturing. If you don’t know something yet, say so.

2

We work out the plan.

I check the calendar, space, and tattoo setup. We agree on how long we need and what the event can accommodate.

3

You reserve the party.

Once we agree on the details, the $250 reservation deposit confirms the date. I’ll let you know exactly how to pay.

4

Your guests sign up.

Start a list with names only. Around two weeks before the party, we’ll check it together and plan the available tattoo slots. I’ll discuss tattoo ideas with guests separately. Later additions depend on time.

Already booked? Use this guest signup sheet template in Google Sheets or Excel, or simply write down names however works for your group.

Have a date in mind?

Send the inquiry and I’ll take it from there. You can come with a full vision or just an idea.
