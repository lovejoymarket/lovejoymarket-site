01$250 reservation depositRequired after approval to reserve the date. It does not cover or reduce anybody’s tattoo cost.

How The Date Becomes Official
1. Send the inquiry.

Share both date options, group size, party type, tattoo plan, logistics, and promotion plan when applicable.

2. LoveJoy reviews the fit.

An inquiry is a request, not a hold. The party details and realistic tattoo capacity have to be approved first.

3. Pay the reservation deposit.

After approval, the $250 deposit makes the agreed date official. It does not cover anybody’s tattoo.

4. Lock the plan.

Final expected tattoo participants and the basic tattoo plan are due at least 14 days before the party.
