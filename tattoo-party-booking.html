What Stays The Same For Both
01$250 reservation depositRequired after approval to reserve the date. It does not cover or reduce anybody’s tattoo cost.
0230 days in advanceTattoo parties must be booked at least 30 days before the requested date.
0314-day final planningFinal expected participant count and basic tattoo plans should be provided at least 14 days before the party.
04$50 tattoo minimum$50 is the minimum per tattoo, not a universal party price. Final pricing varies by the work.

06Capacity is approvedThe party does not include unlimited tattoos. Capacity depends on the party window and the actual tattoo plans.
