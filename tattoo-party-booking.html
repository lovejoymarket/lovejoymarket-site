When The Date Gets Serious
1. Send the inquiry.

Give me two dates, the group size, party type, tattoo ideas, setup details, and the promo plan if there is one.

2. I make sure it works.

The form is a request, not a hold. I need to know the plan fits the date, room, and amount of tattoo time before I say yes.

3. Pay the reservation deposit.

Once I approve it, the $250 deposit makes the date official. It still does not pay for anybody’s tattoo.

4. Give me the final headcount.

The tattoo count and basic ideas are due at least 14 days before the party. Last-minute cousins are not guaranteed a spot.
