Consent Form FAQ
The PDF is not saving my answers.

Download it first instead of filling it inside a browser preview. Use Adobe Acrobat, Apple Files/Preview, or another PDF app, save it, close it, then reopen it to confirm the entries stayed.

I do not understand one of the provisions.

Do not guess. Email hello@lovejoymarket.co before signing so your question can be answered in writing.

What about the government ID upload box?

The PDF labels that area as reserved for a Jotform upload. The downloadable PDF itself cannot upload a file there. Bring your valid government-issued ID to the appointment. If LoveJoy asks you to send an ID copy in advance, follow the instructions in your appointment communication rather than emailing sensitive identification unless specifically requested.

Can I just bring the form with me?

The goal is to have it completed before arrival so appointment time is not used for paperwork. If you are having a technical problem, email before the appointment.
