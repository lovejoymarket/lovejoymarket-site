Tiny Consent Questions
The PDF is not saving my answers.

Download it before you start. Fill it in with Adobe Acrobat, Apple Files or Preview, or another PDF app. Save it, close it, and reopen it to make sure your answers stayed.

I do not understand one of the provisions.

Do not guess and definitely do not initial it anyway. Email hello@lovejoymarket.co before signing so I can answer you in writing.

What about the government ID upload box?

That box came from the original Jotform. The downloadable PDF cannot upload anything there. Bring your valid government-issued ID to the appointment. Do not email sensitive identification unless I specifically ask and give you instructions.

Can I just bring the form with me?

Please finish it before you arrive so we do not spend your appointment doing paperwork. If the file is fighting you, email me before the appointment.
