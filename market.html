How To Apply Without Creating Administrative Folklore
✦★✧♡✦★✧
1
Download the right PDF.

Do not fill it in the browser and then close the tab like a beautiful little disaster. Save the file to your device first.

2
Fill it out + save the completed copy.

Use Adobe Acrobat, Preview, Files, or another PDF app that preserves form fields. Reopen it once to make sure your answers actually saved.

3
Add the receipts.

Portfolio/product photos, booth examples, logo, permits, licenses, insurance, training certificates, food-safety docs, or other current documentation your activity requires.

4
Email one complete submission.

Send the completed PDF and all attachments to hello@lovejoymarket.co. Suggested subject: APPLICATION – type – your name/business.

5
Then wait for an actual yes.

Submitting an application does not reserve a spot, date, wall, booth, tattoo station, or workshop. If a fee applies, you will receive the amount and official payment instructions after acceptance.

Vendor Rules, But Make Them Legible
Applying ≠ accepted.

No booth or event spot exists until LoveJoy confirms it.

Fees vary by event.

Do not send money with an application. If selected, your acceptance tells you the fee, deadline, and official payment method.

Your spot is not transferable.

No selling, gifting, trading, sharing, substituting, or sending your own replacement.

Bring what was approved.

Sell or perform only the products/services/activity LoveJoy approved. Material changes need approval first.

Regulated means documented.

Food, body art, cosmetology, alcohol, or other regulated activities may not proceed until current required documentation is verified.

Keep the room usable.

Stay inside your footprint. Keep exits, stairs, walkways, tattoo areas, and protected/private areas clear.

You run your own checkout.

Vendors handle their own customer payments, receipts, bags, change, and tax obligations unless an event agreement says otherwise.

Leave it cute.

Arrive in your assigned load-in window, keep your area staffed, stay through approved breakdown, and clean/reset your space.

Safety wins arguments.

Harassment, threats, unsafe conduct, illegal products, unapproved flame/alcohol, or refusal to follow safety directions can end participation immediately.

Event-specific instructions can add requirements. Current health, safety, licensing, insurance, lease, and legal requirements always override event convenience.

The Waitlist Is A Real List, Not Purgatory

If the event you want is full, submit the application anyway.

Approved applicants can be placed on the event waitlist. Openings generally move in chronological order among approved applicants, but LoveJoy may move to the applicant who actually fits the opening when category, footprint, power, permit, or other event requirements matter.

more than 24 hrs before doorsan offered vendor will normally have about 2 hours to accept

If you cancel, your spot returns to LoveJoy Market. Do not recruit your own replacement. The opening goes back through the approved waitlist.

Frequently Asked Little Situations
The event says full. Should I still apply?

Yes. If your application is approved, you can be considered for the waitlist if space opens.

Do I pay when I submit?

No. Vendor fees vary by event. A completed application never reserves a space by itself. If you are accepted, LoveJoy sends the event-specific fee and official payment instructions.

Can my friend take my booth if I cancel?

No. Spaces belong to the event and cannot be transferred. Contact LoveJoy immediately and the opening goes back through the waitlist.

I provide a service. What documents do you need?

Attach the current permits, licenses, certifications, insurance, safety training, or other documentation required for the service you plan to perform. If you are not sure what LoveJoy needs to verify, send what you have and explain the activity clearly.

What do food or beverage vendors send?

Start with the vendor application, product/setup images, and the current permits or food-safety documentation applicable to what you plan to serve. Final confirmation can be held until required documents are verified.

Do featured artists pay to hang work?

No. LoveJoy Market does not charge featured artists a participation or display fee. Installation, sales handling, commission if any, and payout timing are agreed before the work goes on the wall.

Can I pitch something that does not fit a category?

Absolutely. Use the Creative + Partner application and explain what you want to do, what you provide, what LoveJoy would need to provide, and any compliance or space needs.

Where do I send general questions?

hello@lovejoymarket.co. Same inbox for questions, forms, attachments, and “this idea is weird but hear me out.”
